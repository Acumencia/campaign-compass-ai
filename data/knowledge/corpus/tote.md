## Capacity and dimensions
The fictional Ridgeline tote holds 12 liters and measures 36 by 30 by 12 centimeters. Capacity is a nominal internal volume, not a certified maximum load.

## Materials
The tote has a cotton canvas body and fixed cotton handles. It has no closure or padded electronics compartment. Do not describe it as providing the protection of a dedicated laptop case.

## Care and price
Spot-clean and air dry. Color transfer testing has not been performed in this synthetic scenario. The demo list price is 30 demo USD and the tote is not eligible for EVERYDAY10.

## Weather limits
No waterproof or water-resistance claim is approved for Ridgeline. Dayline light-splash resistance applies only to Dayline. Carrying a bottle in the tote does not change the tote material rating.

## Selection
Ridgeline may suit open-access carrying needs. Ask whether closure, padding or hands-free carrying is required before choosing it over a pack or sling. No comparative comfort study is available.
