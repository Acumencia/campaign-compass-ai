## Caption structure
Open with a concrete everyday use, name one supported product fact, and finish with an optional invitation. For Dayline, commuting with an 18-liter pack is supported; becoming more productive is not a measured outcome.

## Examples
Approved fictional example: Carry your everyday essentials in the 18-liter Dayline pack. Avoid: The ultimate pack that transforms every commute. Approval covers style only; offer and specification details still need their own current sources.

## Accessibility
Use descriptive language instead of emoji-only instructions. Put essential offer conditions in readable copy. Describe the product in image alt text without adding unverified benefits or demographic assumptions.

## Channel limits
For this exercise, proposed social captions should stay below 240 characters and contain at most one optional hashtag. These are fictional editorial preferences, not platform limits or advertising law.

## Review
A reviewer checks each factual phrase against current specifications and claims policy. A draft is not publication approval. Do not present invented customer quotations as testimonials.
