## Research provenance
Five fictional weekend-walk scenarios were authored for demonstration. They are not survey responses or market research. Counts and quotations must be labeled synthetic if shown.

## Carrying needs
The scenarios include snacks, a layer and a bottle for short walks. None establishes load capacity or medical safety. Product suitability requires checking size, weather and intended use.

## Weather concerns
Two scenarios mention wanting equipment for sustained heavy rain. The current corpus does not establish that Dayline or Ridgeline meets that need. Clarify conditions instead of recommending unsupported protection.

## Messaging hypotheses
A practical, non-technical description may fit occasional walking. Avoid expedition language, rescue claims and promised physical performance improvements. These are fictional brand choices, not legal determinations.

## Limits
No field test, comfort trial or safety assessment occurred. The synthetic scenarios cannot prove durability, wilderness suitability, waterproofing or demand among real hikers.
