## Policy scope
This is an internal policy for the fictional Luma Trail exercise, not legal advice or a statement of real advertising law. It applies alongside the core advertising claims policy.

## Evidence required
Do not claim carbon neutrality, recycled-content percentages or lifecycle benefits without a current product-specific evidence document. No such certification or lifecycle assessment is provided in this corpus.

## Material versus outcome
A stainless steel body is a documented material fact for the matching bottle. That fact alone does not prove lower emissions, indefinite reuse, recyclability in every locality or an environmental advantage.

## Rejected example
Rejected fictional caption: Our carbon-neutral bottle saves the planet. This is a negative example, not an approved claim. Retrieval of this sentence must not convert it into usable product evidence.

## Allowed approach
Use current material and care facts without adding environmental outcomes. If a user requests a sustainability claim beyond the available evidence, state the evidence gap and request human review.
