## Research provenance
Four fictional gifting situations were authored to test recommendation questions. They are not customer data. None supplies age-specific product safety evidence.

## Budget needs
Two scenarios set a 40 demo USD merchandise budget before shipping. A product below that price may still exceed a delivered budget. Calculate shipping using current policy and disclose assumptions.

## Unknown preferences
When capacity, recipient needs or care preferences are unknown, ask a clarifying question. Do not infer that a smaller bottle is suitable for children or that a tote suits every recipient.

## Offer limits
Gift status does not extend EVERYDAY10 to Mini, Waypoint or Ridgeline. An eligible standard Switchback or Dayline item must still satisfy the campaign terms.

## Recommendation limits
There is no best gift ranking, personalization service or gift-wrap price in this corpus. A helpful answer may identify missing information rather than invent a recommendation.
