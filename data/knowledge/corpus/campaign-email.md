## Scope and provenance
The fictional September 2026 email exercise used 1000 delivered messages. No actual recipients were contacted, and no real email service data was collected.

## Observed metrics
The authored example records 80 unique clicks and 12 attributed purchases. Click-through rate on delivered messages is 8 percent. There is no reliable open-count field in this scenario.

## Attribution
Purchases use a fictional three-day click window. Delivered-message click rate is not interchangeable with ad-impression CTR. No spend field is available, so cost per acquisition and ROAS cannot be calculated.

## Creative
The subject-line scenario emphasized everyday carrying needs. There was no randomized subject-line test. The observed count cannot prove that the subject caused purchases.

## Learning
A proposed next test would randomize a clearly documented subject-line variation. Keep factual product claims identical across arms and establish attribution before interpreting results.
