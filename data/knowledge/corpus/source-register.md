## Conflict handling
If two active sources disagree on the same scoped fact, identify the conflict and abstain instead of choosing a convenient passage. A later ingestion timestamp alone does not establish a later effective policy.

## Evaluation boundary
Citation quote matching verifies that text exists in a supplied passage. It does not establish that the passage supports the generated claim. Human review must check meaning, product scope and effective version.
