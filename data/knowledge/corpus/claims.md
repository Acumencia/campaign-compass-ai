## Approved and prohibited claims
Use only documented product capacities, weights, materials, care instructions and demo prices. Neither Switchback nor Dayline may be described as waterproof, carbon neutral, medically beneficial or guaranteed to improve performance. The Switchback bottle has no approved temperature-retention duration. The Dayline pack may be described as resisting light splashes, with the limitation that it is not waterproof.
## Evidence and creative language
Factual campaign statements must cite their product specification or policy section. Creative slogans are proposals requiring human review, not facts or endorsements. Do not invent testimonials, scarcity, comparative superiority or certifications. A product fact in one document does not authorize claims about another product.
