## Subject lines
Use a specific product or use case in subject lines, with a fictional editorial target of 45 characters or fewer. Avoid false reply prefixes, invented scarcity, and guaranteed outcomes.

## Offer disclosure
When mentioning EVERYDAY10, include its October 31, 2026 end date and non-stacking condition in the body. Do not imply that shipping is discounted. The campaign and shipping documents govern exact eligibility.

## Examples
A style example is Everyday space for your commute. This describes a theme, not evidence of increased productivity. Any numerical capacity must come from the matching product specification.

## Audience adaptation
For fictional commuter research, emphasize carrying needs and care. For occasional day hikers, explain product limitations. Do not imply that the same message was proven to work for both groups.

## Review
Link factual claims to source sections in the internal draft. A human reviews tone, product scope and offer dates before release. This application does not send marketing emails.
