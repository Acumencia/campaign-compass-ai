## ROAS
Observed ROAS equals attributed revenue divided by advertising spend when spend is positive. It excludes non-ad costs and does not establish incremental revenue. State the attribution window alongside a comparison.

## Acquisition cost
Cost per attributed purchase equals spend divided by attributed purchases when purchases are positive. If purchases are zero or missing, the ratio is undefined; do not replace it with zero.

## Rate denominators
Ad CTR uses clicks divided by impressions. Email delivered-message click rate uses unique clicks divided by delivered messages. Different denominators must be named and should not be silently compared.

## Causal limits
Observational differences may reflect audience, season, offer or attribution differences. A controlled design and uncertainty analysis are needed before making causal claims. The fictional reports do not establish real effects.

## Dataset separation
Luma Trail narrative campaign reports and campaigns.csv are independent synthetic datasets. Never join them on similar channel names or dates. Missing identity and measurement links make such a combination unsupported.
